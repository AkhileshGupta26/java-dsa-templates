What is Kruskal's Algorithm?

Goal:
Find Minimum Spanning Tree (MST)

Requirements:

1. Connect all nodes
2. No cycles
3. Minimum total weight

Core Idea
Instead of growing a tree like Prim,

Kruskal says:
Take the cheapest edge available.

Then:
If it creates a cycle → Skip
Otherwise → Take it

Repeat

Why DSU is Perfect Here
Cycle detection becomes:
if(find(u)==find(v))
That's O(1) amortized.
Without DSU we'd need DFS every edge.
